02How pages update

The page changes when the job closes. Not when someone gets round to it.

Each runbook page is a plain file. A closed job edits the page it used, the old version stays on file, and the edit carries the job number.

04Setup

Connect your job records once. Every new team starts from there.

Ferrow finds your work orders and tickets, strips out duplicates and test jobs, and clears who may read what. Add a second team or a new region and that work is already done.

05Access

Each person sees the pages their job needs.

A dispatcher can open customer records. Nobody in the field can open HR files. Ferrow checks every request, from a person or a bot, against the scope you set.

Read how access works

  • Scopes per role

    Set once what techs, agents and drivers may read or edit. Bots get the same rules.

  • A log you can export

    See who opened or changed a page, and which job made the change.

  • Undo any edit

    Old versions stay on file, so a bad change rolls back in one click.

06Teams

Where crews put it to work.

Fix it on the first visit.

A tech finds the real cause on a repeat call and closes the job. Ferrow adds the step to that unit's page, and the next crew sees it on the tablet before they leave the yard.

Answer with the current fix.

Tickets come in one after another. Each agent opens the page for the issue and sees the fix that closed the last ticket like it, with a link to that ticket.

Send it to the right depot.

When one depot finds a faster way to sort returns or load a van, a driver logs it on the run. Ferrow puts it on that depot's page, and every hub that sends parcels there works from the same steps.

Try it with one crew for 30 days.
